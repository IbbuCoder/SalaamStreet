When mounting/boarding for travel

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَى رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ

Subhanal-ladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila Rabbina lamunqalibun.

Glory be to the One who subjected this for us, for we could never have done so ourselves — and indeed, to our Lord we shall return.

Source: Qur'an 43:13–14; Sahih Muslim 1342 (travel dua)
