7. Privacy

  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • As a guest, your bookmarks, progress, settings and logs stay on your device.
  • An anonymous daily count. To know how many people use SalaamStreet, each device adds 1 to that day's total the first time it opens the app (as a guest or signed in). Only the day and the total are kept — no identifier, IP address or anything about your device — and your device isn't counted at all if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • With an account, we store your email address (or phone number) and sign-in method, and the items you choose to sync. Your location is never synced.
  • Closed-app reminders, if you turn them on, store your device's push address, your location rounded to about 1 km, your time zone and reminder settings so reminders can be sent.
  • SalaamStreet AI: your questions, the conversation so far and — only if “Use my app data” is on — a short summary of your SalaamStreet data are sent to Google's Gemini API to be answered, under Google's terms for that service. Ayah, tafsir and hadith text from the app that goes with your question is sent too. SalaamStreet doesn't store your conversations (“Save” downloads a copy to your own device only, and “Read aloud” uses your device's own voice). We do store, for each request, counts, token use, estimated cost and errors, and whether you rated the answer helpful or not; your off-topic warnings and any pause; and when you accepted these terms. Refused requests are logged (who and why, never what you wrote).
  • Community Rules: we record when you agreed to them, and any warning, ban (its reason and message) or appeal (what you wrote in it and the reply), so that admins can be fair and consistent. Admins see counts of what your account uses, never your synced notes or bookmarks. Every admin action is logged.
  • We use trusted providers to run the Service, including Supabase (accounts and database), Google (Gemini AI and Google sign-in), Apple (sign-in), AlQuran Cloud and AlAdhan (Qur'an text and prayer times), and Google Fonts. They receive only what they need.
  • You can delete your synced data and your account at any time from the Account page. Data you keep only on your device is removed when you clear the site's data.
