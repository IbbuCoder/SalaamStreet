The story behind SalaamStreet

Version 2.11.8

SalaamStreet didn't start as an app. When Ibrahim was 12, it was an Islamic-themed online shop he built on Shopify. It didn't take off. His next attempt didn't either.

But Ibrahim kept the name and changed the idea. Instead of selling things to Muslims, what if SalaamStreet gave them something — every day, for free?

Today Ibrahim is a student at Neuqua Valley High School, where he's part of the Muslim Student Association.

SalaamStreet is one calm place to find prayer times, face the Qibla, read and listen to the Qur'an, learn authentic duas, track your prayers, start learning Arabic and remember Allah — with no ads, no account required, and your data staying on your device unless you choose to sign in and sync it.

Ibrahim had the idea; his father, Aquil, helped bring it to life.

Our promise

  • Everything you need stays free, forever. Prayer times, Qibla, the Qur'an, duas, dhikr, the prayer tracker, Arabic learning and syncing across your devices will never be behind a paywall.
  • Paid extras will only ever be for things that genuinely cost money to provide — like an AI tutor or live courses — and you'll be able to choose a subscription or pay once and own it forever.
  • One day, the SalaamStreet shop may return too — while the app itself stays free.

    • Only MSA admins can post announcements — and admins manage members too

  3. Version 2.11.6

    NVHS MSA members

    Join the NVHS MSA in the app — with the code shown at a meeting, or by asking — and see members-only announcements.

    Join the MSA

    • “Join the NVHS MSA” on the MSA page: type your first and last name, plus the meeting code if you're at a meeting
    • The meeting code is 6 digits and changes every 10 minutes, so it only works if you're in the room — enter it and you're in straight away
    • No code? Send your name and the MSA's approver checks it against the member list
    • One account per name: if a name already belongs to a member, the request is flagged and you're asked to see Ibrahim in person
    • Posters can mark an announcement “Members only” — for rooms, times and photos; nobody else receives it
    • Membership lasts until the end of the school year (1 July); everyone re-joins in the fall

    For the MSA's approver

    • A Manage page with the live meeting code and a countdown to the next one
    • Requests with “On the roster” or “Not on the roster” and the account that asked; approve, deny or remove in one tap; flagged ones first
    • Paste the roster from Google Classroom; it's kept only in the private database, never in the app's code
  4. Version 2.11.5

    Modes, made useful

    Each mode now answers one question the moment you open the app — and switching takes one tap.

    Every mode has a point

    • Travel Mode tells you how to pray: Dhuhr, Asr and Isha as 2 rak'ahs, and when you may combine — with sources, right in the prayer card on Home
    • Travel Mode: set the day you're coming home; after it, Home asks once and “I'm home” switches the mode off and your prayer times back to home
    • Mosque Mode: one big “I'm at the mosque” button turns on Quiet Mode for an hour, and it ends by itself
    • Hajj & Umrah Mode: your next step on Home, and a round counter for tawaf and sa'i that says where each round goes

    Cleaner and easier

    • No more box of grey tiles above Home: the mode's answer sits inside the prayer card, and Home gets shorter
    • Tap the mode badge in the top bar to switch modes without leaving the page
    • A simpler mode picker: one row per mode, one tap, and a line saying what each is for
    • The app's own icons and a colour for each mode, instead of emoji
    • Kids Mode is set up from the Family page (linked from Modes), since it belongs to a child's device

    Fixes

    • If your location can't be found, Travel Mode and Mosque Mode say so instead of loading forever
    • On a Friday afternoon, Mosque Mode shows next Friday's Jumu'ah, not today's
    • Long Names of Allah no longer push the Name of the day card off the edge of small phones

  6. Version 2.10.0

    Modes

    A new way to experience SalaamStreet: five Modes that put the right tools first for what you're doing — at home, travelling, learning as a child, on Hajj or Umrah, or at the mosque.

    New: Modes

    • Choose Your Mode — Normal, Travel, Kids, Hajj & Umrah and Mosque — from your Account page, the menu or the sidebar
    • Each mode puts its own dashboard at the top of Home and reorders your quick links; switching back to Normal restores everything, and nothing is ever deleted
    • Your mode is remembered after a refresh or restart, and syncs across your devices when you're signed in; guests keep it on the device and bring it with them when they sign up
    • A small mode badge in the top bar opens the mode picker; gentle, dismissible suggestions (at most one per visit) offer a mode on the Mosques, Calendar and travel-dua pages

    Kids Mode and Family

    • A parent-managed Kids Mode: add several children (first name or nickname, age range and avatar only) and choose what each can use
    • Children never get the parent's password: a device is connected to one child with a one-time code, or by the parent on their own device, and a grown-up PIN is needed to leave
    • Learn prayer and wudu, read and listen to short surahs, everyday duas, stories of the Prophets, Names of Allah, good manners, pillars and history — every lesson with its source
    • Quizzes, put-the-steps-in-order activities, flashcards, a daily learning card and gentle achievements — saved for each child separately, never a competition
    • A Family page for parents: each child's real progress, achievements and devices; restrictions enforced by the server; reset progress or remove a child and all their data

    Travel Mode

    • Prayer times with a countdown, the Qibla and the Hijri date wherever you are — with or without location permission
    • A destination with its own prayer times and Qibla, saved places, and one tap to use a place for prayer times app-wide (and back home again)
    • Travel duas, a four-part travel checklist, and sourced guidance on shortening and combining prayers that sets out where the schools differ

    Hajj & Umrah Mode

    • Step-by-step Umrah (7 steps) and Hajj (11 steps, 8–13 Dhul-Hijjah) guides with sources, the duas for each step, and notes where scholars differ
    • Mark steps done, see where you are and what's next, resume where you left off, and start over for a new pilgrimage; a packing and preparation checklist

    Mosque Mode

    • Choose your mosque near you or by searching an area, save favourites, and see its prayer times, next Friday and Qibla
    • Honest data: details come from OpenStreetMap and are labelled as such; times are calculated start times, not the mosque's iqamah; you can note a Jumu'ah time for yourself
    • Optional Quiet Mode: no animations, fewer cards, and no non-prayer reminders or sounds from SalaamStreet while you're there

    Improvements

    • Everything in Modes works on phones, tablets and desktops, in light and dark themes, in all seven interface languages, with keyboard and screen-reader support
    • Mode pages load only when opened, and keep working offline once visited
    • The mosque finder now also shows a mosque's website and phone when OpenStreetMap has them
