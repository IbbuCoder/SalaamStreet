# SalaamStreet 2.11.0 — NVHS MSA

*A home for the Neuqua Valley High School Muslim Student Association.*

Release 2.11.0 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** the MSA page needs the new database tables and
> functions. Run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` in the Supabase SQL
> editor (it is safe to re-run). Until then the MSA page shows an error with
> "Try again", Home shows no MSA card, and everything else works. See
> [`backend/README-backend.md`](../../backend/README-backend.md#31--nvhs-msa-announcements).

## New: the NVHS MSA page

- **NVHS MSA** in the sidebar (under Community) and in the phone's More menu.
- The MSA's announcements, newest first, with pinned ones at the top. Each can
  have a picture; long announcements fold behind "Read more"; web links can be
  tapped.
- No account is needed to read. The last announcements loaded stay available
  offline.
- Important announcements also appear as **one slim card on everyone's Home**
  (with the picture as a thumbnail). It opens the announcement, and the × hides
  it for good on that device.
- Announcements can have an end date (1 day, 1 week, 1 month, or none); after
  it they disappear for everyone.

## Posting (MSA posters only)

- The MSA's posters sign in with their own account. A **New** button then
  appears on the MSA page, and **Edit** and **Delete** on each announcement.
- The form has a title, text, an optional photo (with Change / Remove), "Show on
  everyone's Home", "Pin to the top" and "Show for". It reminds posters to post
  photos of students only with their permission.
- Photos are shrunk on the poster's device and re-drawn as a JPEG before they
  are uploaded, which also removes camera and location data.

## Safety

- Only accounts on the MSA's poster list can post, edit or delete — and the
  database checks it on every write, not just the app. The list holds SHA-256
  hashes of the email addresses, never the addresses (the code is public).
- Nothing is reachable through the database's table API; reading and writing
  go through functions. Pictures must be JPEG, WebP or PNG data and are
  size-limited by the database; at most 30 new posts an hour.
- Kids Mode never shows MSA announcements.

## Tests

- `tests/msa.test.js` — 5 database tests in real Postgres: posters post, edit
  and delete; everyone reads; nobody else can write (signed in or not), and the
  tables can't be read or changed directly; adding a poster by email; input
  checks and expiry.
- `tests/e2e.test.js` — 1 new browser test: a poster posts with a photo, a guest
  sees it on Home and the MSA page and can hide the Home card, another account
  gets no buttons and is refused by the database, and the poster edits and
  deletes.
