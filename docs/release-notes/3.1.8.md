# SalaamStreet 3.1.8 — Steadier Qibla camera, MSA admins

*A mini update: the Qibla camera stops jumping, and the NVHS MSA runs itself.*

Release 3.1.8 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` in the
> Supabase SQL editor (safe to re-run). Until then, the Manage page's new
> parts (students, admins, "approve all") show errors; everything else works.

## Qibla camera fixes

- **No more spinning on iPhone.** Held upright, an iPhone's orientation angles
  flip by 180° as it passes vertical, while its compass doesn't. The app used
  to read that as a 180° error and slowly swung the view round. It now
  compares the compass with the phone's real 3-D direction, averages the
  correction slowly (the compass is noisy, the gyroscope isn't), holds it in
  the half-tilted zone where iOS switches between the top edge and the
  camera, and only believes a big jump once it has lasted half a second.
- **No more flicker.** Each part of the camera view changes only past a
  margin: "Turn left 23°" is rewritten when the number moves 2° or more (or
  after half a second), the Kaaba marker doesn't blink at the edge of the
  view, the big arrow doesn't swap sides when the Qibla is right behind you,
  and "Raise your phone" doesn't toggle on and off near flat. Compass mode's
  switch between the top edge and the camera has the same margin.
- **Camera mode from a link or after a reload** now gets the Qibla on the
  heading strip and the degrees chip once the location arrives (they used to
  stay missing).
- The camera view says when the **compass needs calibrating**.

## NVHS MSA admins

- **One list of admins.** Only admins post announcements, and admins manage
  members. The MSA's two owners are admins and can't be removed in the app;
  the posters from earlier versions were removed once on upgrade.
- **Add and remove admins** by email on Manage → Admins. You can't remove an
  owner or yourself.
- **Add a student** by name — and, if you like, the email they sign in with.
  With an email, they're a member as soon as they sign in (any email, not
  just school ones); if they already have an account, straight away.

## A better Manage page

- At a glance: members, waiting, how much of the list joined, and **who
  joined this week**.
- **Approve all on the list**: one tap approves everyone waiting whose name is
  on the MSA's list (never a name another member already has).
- **New announcement** right from Manage, and **Download list (CSV)** —
  made on your device.
- The list: **All / Joined / Not yet** filters with counts, search by name
  or email, the email each student signs in with, and one tap to take a name
  off the list.
