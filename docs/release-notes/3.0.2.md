# SalaamStreet 3.0.2 — Community Rules

*Rules everyone agrees to, bans that work at once and tell people why, and appeals.*

Release 3.0.2 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` (safe to
> re-run) and deploy the `admin` and `ai-lab` Edge Functions — GitHub →
> Actions → "Deploy Supabase" does all three.

## Why

In 3.0.1 a ban was saved, but a banned person's device kept working for up to
an hour and was never told why; "Sign out everywhere" had the same gap. There
were no written rules to point to, and no way to appeal.

## Community Rules

Seven rules, on the Terms page (`terms/#rules`) and in the app:

1. Keep your name and email respectful.
2. Use SalaamStreet AI respectfully (nothing hateful or sexual, nothing that mocks Islam).
3. Use SalaamStreet AI for Islamic questions and the app; don't trick it.
4. Don't pretend to be someone else.
5. Keep NVHS MSA posts appropriate.
6. One account per person.
7. Don't hack or abuse the app.

- **Before signing in** (every method) there's a required box: "I've read and
  agree to the Terms of Use and the Community Rules."
- **Everyone already signed in** is asked once on their next visit (agree, or
  sign out). The version and date are recorded (`profiles.rules_version`,
  `rules_accepted_at`); bump `RULES_VERSION` in `js/standing.js` (and
  `RULES_UPDATED` in `tools/build-pages.js`) to ask everyone again.

## Ban reasons

Every ban and warning uses one of these (`public.ban_reasons()`), shown to the
person in their own language: offensive name or email · hateful, sexual or
disrespectful use of SalaamStreet AI · repeatedly breaking SalaamStreet AI's
rules · impersonation · inappropriate MSA posts · extra or fake accounts ·
hacking or abusing the app · other (the admin writes why).

## What a banned person sees

- The device checks its standing when SalaamStreet opens, when it comes back
  to the front and every 5 minutes. A banned account is signed out on that
  device at once, and a screen shows: the reason, the admin's message (if
  any), when it ends (or "until the ban is lifted"), the Community Rules and an
  **Appeal** form. "Continue without an account" closes it — everything free
  still works.
- The server refuses everything from a banned account straight away, even
  with a sign-in token that hasn't expired: syncing (row-level security),
  NVHS MSA actions and SalaamStreet AI.
- A banned account can't sign in any more, so the device keeps a copy of its
  last sign-in token (only on that device) and uses it to read the ban and to
  appeal (`account_notice`, `account_appeal`). Nobody can look up anyone
  else's ban. Signing out on purpose forgets the copy.
- Trying to sign in to a banned account says it's banned, and to reload on a
  device that was signed in to see why.
- **Appeals:** one at a time, up to 3 per ban. The admin's reply shows on the
  ban screen. Lifting the ban (unban, or accepting an appeal) also unblocks the
  email if the ban blocked it.

## Sign out everywhere

Ends every session; devices still open are signed out at once (their next
check sees the ended session) and every request from them is refused.

## Admin Panel

- **In the More menu and the sidebar**, only for admins, with a red count of
  flags and appeals waiting (the Settings card is gone).
- **Ban…** and **Warn…** open a form: reason, how long, a message they'll see,
  a private note, and "also block the email from making a new account".
- **Appeals** tab: waiting and answered appeals with the ban's details; lift
  the ban or turn it down, with a reply.
- **Blocked emails** (in Flags): block an email by hand or unblock one.
- **Bulk actions** in Users: tick accounts, then warn, ban or dismiss flags.
- **CSV downloads** of users (current search and filter) and of the log.
- **Banner** tab: a short notice on everyone's Home (information, important or
  celebration), with an optional end time; people can hide it.
- Overview, Flags and Appeals refresh themselves every 30 seconds.
