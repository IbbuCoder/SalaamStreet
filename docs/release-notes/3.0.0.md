# SalaamStreet 3.0 — Modes

*A new way to experience SalaamStreet.*

Release 3.0.0 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** Kids Mode and the Family page need the new database
> tables and functions. Run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` in the
> Supabase SQL editor (it is safe to re-run). Until then, everything else in
> 3.0 works, and the Family page shows an error with "Try again" instead of
> data. See [`backend/README-backend.md`](../../backend/README-backend.md).

## New: Modes

- Added **Choose Your Mode**, reached from the Modes card on your Account page
  ("Your SalaamStreet"), the sidebar, and the phone's More menu.
- Five modes: **Normal**, **Travel**, **Kids**, **Hajj & Umrah** and **Mosque**.
- Each mode adds its own dashboard to the top of Home, reorders the quick links
  and steps back the Home cards it doesn't need. Switching back to Normal
  restores the standard Home; switching never deletes anything.
- The active mode is remembered across refreshes and restarts. Signed in, it
  syncs across your devices; as a guest it stays on the device and is carried
  into your account when you sign up. Unknown or damaged values fall back to
  Normal.
- A small mode badge in the top bar shows the current mode and opens the picker.
- Gentle suggestions — never automatic switches — on the Mosques page (Mosque
  Mode), the Calendar (Hajj & Umrah Mode) and the travel duas (Travel Mode). At
  most one per visit; "Not now" hides that suggestion for 30 days.

## Kids Mode and Family

- A **parent-managed** Kids Mode using the parent's existing SalaamStreet
  account — no second login system.
- **+ Add Kid**: a first name or nickname, an age range (4–6, 7–9, 10–12) and an
  avatar. Nothing else is collected. Parents confirm they are the parent or
  guardian. Up to 10 children per family.
- **Secure child access:** a child's device is connected to one child with a
  one-time, 8-character code (valid 15 minutes), or the parent starts Kids Mode
  on their own device — which signs the parent out of that device first, so a
  child never holds the parent's session or password. A grown-up PIN (4–8
  digits, stored only as a salted hash on the device, with lock-out after
  repeated wrong tries) is needed to leave Kids Mode or switch child. A parent
  who forgets the PIN signs in to their account to leave.
- **Multiple children**, each with separate progress, achievements and
  devices; siblings can share a tablet, and switching child needs the PIN.
- **Learning:** prayer basics, rak'ahs, wudu and how to pray (with
  put-the-steps-in-order activities); short surahs to read and listen to (text
  from the app's Qur'an source); 10 everyday duas (read, listen with the
  device's voice where available, hide-the-words practice); stories of the
  Prophets chosen by age; Names of Allah flashcards; good manners, the pillars,
  articles of faith and Islamic history; five quizzes. Every lesson shows its
  source.
- **Achievements:** First Dua Learned, First Quiz Completed, First Surah
  Completed, First Story Heard, Prayer Basics Completed, Wudu Basics Completed,
  Five Lessons Completed — awarded by the server from saved progress. No
  rankings, streak pressure or rewards for spending.
- **Family page for parents:** each child's real progress by section, best
  quiz scores, achievements, recent activity and devices; edit a child; remove
  a device; reset progress; remove a child (deletes all of their data
  immediately).
- **Parental controls:** which sections each child can use, whether the child
  sees their own progress, and whether recitation audio plays. Switched-off
  sections are hidden *and* refused by the database.
- **Safety:** Kids Mode has no chat, posting, public profiles, community
  content, ads, sharing or links out of the Kids area, and sends no
  notifications. Learning saved offline is kept on the device and sent when
  the connection returns.

## Travel Mode

- Prayer times with a countdown, the Qibla and the Hijri date for where you
  are — using the app's existing location choices. Nothing asks for location by
  itself; without permission you can search for a place by name.
- A destination with its own prayer times (local time there) and Qibla, saved
  places, and one tap to use a place for prayer times across the whole app
  (and to switch back home). Places stay on the device.
- Travel duas (Sahih Muslim 1342, 2708 and more) and a four-part checklist
  (before leaving, during, at the destination, returning) that remembers your
  ticks and can be reset.
- Guidance on shortening (qasr), combining, who counts as a traveller, praying
  behind a resident imam, Jumu'ah, voluntary prayers, praying on planes and
  trains, wiping over footwear and fasting — each with sources, with the main
  differences between the schools stated, and a reminder to ask a trusted
  scholar.
- Offline: guidance, duas and the checklist work offline; prayer times are
  calculated on the device without a connection; finding a location or a new
  place needs location access or a connection. The page labels which is which.

## Hajj & Umrah Mode

- Asks "What are you preparing for?" — Hajj or Umrah — and lets you change
  later; progress is kept separately for each.
- **Umrah guide (7 steps):** preparation, ihram, the miqat, the talbiyah,
  tawaf, sa'i, and shaving/shortening.
- **Hajj guide (11 steps):** preparation and the three types of Hajj, arriving
  in Makkah, the 8th and Mina, Arafah, Muzdalifah, stoning Jamrat al-Aqabah, the
  sacrifice, shaving and the first release, Tawaf al-Ifadah and sa'i, the days
  in Mina, and the farewell tawaf.
- Each step shows its sources (mainly Sahih Muslim 1218 and other cited
  reports), the duas for that step, and where scholars differ — for example
  the type of Hajj, Muzdalifah, the order of the rites on the 10th, the
  release from ihram, the nights in Mina, and the farewell tawaf.
- Mark steps done, see the current and next step, resume where you left off,
  and start over for a new pilgrimage. A preparation and packing checklist.
  Signed in, progress syncs; as a guest it stays on the device.

## Mosque Mode

- Find mosques near you (with permission) or by searching an area, filter by
  name, choose one and save favourites. Chosen and saved mosques stay on the
  device.
- The mosque's name, address and — when OpenStreetMap has them — website and
  phone, clearly labelled as OpenStreetMap data not verified by the mosque.
- Prayer times for the mosque's location, labelled as calculated start times
  (not the mosque's iqamah); the next Friday's Dhuhr time; and a Jumu'ah time
  you can note for yourself, always shown as your note.
- Announcements and events: an honest empty state — SalaamStreet has no
  verified mosque announcements yet (planned for 3.4).
- The Qibla from the mosque, mosque duas and etiquette with sources.
- **Quiet Mode** (optional): turns off animations, hides extra Home cards, and
  holds back SalaamStreet's own non-prayer reminders and sounds (prayer
  reminders still arrive, silently). It cannot silence the phone or other
  apps, and says so.

## Improvements

- Every Modes screen works on phones, tablets and desktops (checked at 320,
  390, 820 and 1440 px wide), in light and dark themes, and in all seven
  interface languages (Urdu, Bengali, Indonesian, Turkish and French are drafts
  pending native review; long guide and lesson texts are in English and
  Arabic).
- Keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, visible focus, reduced motion,
  44 px+ touch targets, accessible form errors, and selected states shown in
  words and icons, not colour alone.
- Mode pages download only when first opened and keep working offline
  afterwards.
- The mosque finder now also returns a mosque's website and phone when
  OpenStreetMap has them.

## Known limitations

- Mosque information comes only from OpenStreetMap: no iqamah or Jumu'ah
  times, announcements or events from mosques (planned: 3.4 Community).
- Kids Mode sends no notifications and there are no parent-set learning
  reminders yet (planned: 3.8).
- A child's device learns that a parent removed it the next time it contacts
  the server (opening the app, saving progress, or after a minute).
- The Kids Mode lock is an interface lock on a shared device; the security
  boundary is the server. Because the parent is signed out of a device in Kids
  Mode, leaving Kids Mode on it gives only the guest app.
- "Listen" for duas uses the device's text-to-speech voice when an Arabic voice
  is installed; there is no recorded dua audio.
- Travel destinations and saved mosques don't sync between devices (location
  privacy).
