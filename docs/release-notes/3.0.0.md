## Answers built on verified text

- **Ayahs you mention come with their text.** Write "2:255" (up to three
  ayahs per question) and the app's own Arabic and translation of each are
  sent with the question as *verified text from the app*, so the assistant
  quotes them exactly instead of from memory. Times like "at 5:30" or
  "4:30 pm" are left alone.
- **`/tafsir 2:255`** — Tafsir Ibn Kathir (abridged, the same text as the
  Qur'an reader's tafsir sheet) explained in plain language, with what comes
  from the tafsir kept apart from the assistant's own explanation.
- **`/hadith nawawi 1`** — any hadith from the app's 10 collections
  (`bukhari 1`, `abu dawud 7`, …) explained, with its gradings as the app
  lists them. `/hadith` on its own lists the collections and their ranges.
- **"Verified text from the app"** under each answer shows exactly what was
  sent, with "Open in the Qur'an" / "Open in Hadith". Verified text stays
  with the conversation, so follow-up questions can still quote it.
- **Ayah links.** References in answers ("Al-Baqarah 2:255", "(18:10)") open
  that ayah in the Qur'an; coming back keeps the conversation.
- **From the tafsir sheet:** "Ask SalaamStreet AI about this ayah" opens
  SalaamStreet AI with `/tafsir s:a` in the box. Nothing is sent until you
  press Send. (Hidden in Kids Mode and where accounts aren't set up.)

Verified text is sent separately from what you typed (`sources`, up to 8
texts and 16,000 characters), so it never uses up your message length. The
server tells the assistant it is data, never instructions.

## Easier to use

- **Follow-up suggestions** under the latest answer: "Explain it more
  simply", "What's the evidence for this?", "Is there a dua for this?",
  "Sum it up in 3 points".
- **Read aloud** any answer with your device's own voice (tap again to stop).
- **Save** downloads the conversation, with its verified texts, as a `.txt`
  file. It's kept only on your device; SalaamStreet still stores no
  conversations.
- **👍 / 👎 — was it helpful?** Only the number is stored, on that request's
  usage row, for a day after the answer; tap again to take it back.
- **Commands finish themselves.** Typing `/ta` narrows the command chips;
  Tab finishes the command, and Enter finishes and runs one that needs
  nothing more (`/hel` → `/help`).
- On phones, Save and Clear are icon buttons so the conversation header fits
  on one line.

## Out of beta

- No more "Beta" badge or wording. SalaamStreet AI stays **free for everyone
  with an account**, with the same daily allowance and fair waiting list.
- The Lab's beta switch is now "Open to everyone with an account" (the
  database column is still `beta_open`), and its Status card shows
  **Helpful · 7 days** (👍 / 👎 counts).

## Not in this release

- Paid plans (Plus, Pro, Max) and payments — billing stays off
  (`backend/functions/ai-lab/plans.js`), and nothing free has a new limit.
- The AI page is in English (answers follow the language you write in).
