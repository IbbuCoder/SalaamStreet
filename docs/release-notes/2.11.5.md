# SalaamStreet 2.11.5 — Modes, made useful

*Every mode now answers one question the moment you open the app.*

Release 2.11.5 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

No database changes. Design notes: [`docs/06-modes.md`](../06-modes.md) §5.

## Every mode has a point

- **Travel Mode — "How do I pray today, here?"** Home's prayer card shows the
  rak'ahs while travelling — Fajr 2, Dhuhr 2, Asr 2, Maghrib 3, Isha 2, with the
  shortened ones marked — and that you may combine Dhuhr with Asr and Maghrib
  with Isha if you need to, with a link to the sources and where scholars
  differ. The Travel page leads with the same answer and its sources (Qur'an
  4:101 · Sahih Muslim 686 · Sahih al-Bukhari 1107).
- **Your trip:** set the day you're coming home. After it, Home asks "Back
  home?" once; **I'm home** turns Travel Mode off and, if you'd switched prayer
  times to your destination, switches them back to home. **Still travelling**
  keeps it on.
- **Mosque Mode — "Is my phone ready for the mosque?"** One big **I'm at the
  mosque** button (on Home and on the Mosque page) turns on Quiet Mode for an
  hour; it shows until when and ends by itself, or tap **End**. Your mosque and
  its Jumu'ah are one tap below.
- **Hajj & Umrah Mode — "What do I do next?"** Home shows your next step
  ("Umrah · next step, 5 of 7 — Tawaf"). A new **round counter** for tawaf and
  sa'i: one big button to tap after each round, seven dots that fill in, what
  each round is (for sa'i, Safa → Marwah or back; for tawaf, where to start and
  when men walk briskly), undo and start again, and what to do when the seven
  are done (Sahih Muslim 1218). The count stays on the device if you leave the
  page.

## Cleaner and easier

- No more box of grey tiles above Home: the mode's answer sits inside the prayer
  card, so Home gets shorter. Modes also step back the "Your week" card.
- Tap the mode badge in the top bar to switch modes in a small sheet — one tap,
  and you stay on the page you were on.
- A simpler mode picker: one row per mode, a line saying what each is for, and
  one tap to use it.
- The app's own icons with a colour for each mode, instead of emoji.
- Kids Mode is set up from the Family page, which the Modes page links to — it
  belongs to a child's device, not to the person switching modes.
- Mode pages lead with a one-line description of what the mode does; the Hajj
  guide no longer repeats the prayer time and Qibla already on Home.

## Fixes

- If your location can't be found, Travel Mode's prayer times and Mosque Mode's
  "Mosques near me" now say so instead of loading forever.
- On a Friday afternoon, Mosque Mode shows next Friday's Jumu'ah instead of the
  one that has already happened.
- The Quiet Mode switch only appears while Mosque Mode is on (where it works).
- Long Names of Allah (such as Dhul-Jalali wal-Ikram) no longer push Home's
  Name of the day card off the edge of small phones.

## Tests

- `tests/e2e.test.js` — the Modes tests now cover the new picker and quick
  switcher, the rak'ah answer and the trip's "I'm home" (prayer times return
  home), the timed Quiet Mode ending by itself, and the tawaf/sa'i counter; the
  layout test also covers the counter and the MSA page at four widths in light
  and dark.
