# SalaamStreet 3.0.5 — Analytics for admins

Release 3.0.5 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` (safe to
> re-run) — GitHub → Actions → "Deploy Supabase" — and redeploy the `admin`
> Edge Function (new `analytics` action).

- **An anonymous daily visit count.** The first time SalaamStreet opens each
  day (UTC), the device adds 1 to that day's total in `public.app_visits`, as
  a `guest` (signed out) or an `account` (signed in), through
  `visit_count(kind)` (js/standing.js, once a day per device, remembered in
  `localStorage` as `ss-visit-day`). Only the day, the kind and the total are
  kept: no id, IP address or device details. Devices that send Do Not Track or
  Global Privacy Control don't count. Days older than 400 are dropped. The
  Terms (7. Privacy), the facts page and llms.txt say so.
- **Admin Panel → Analytics** (new tab, second in line; `admin_analytics`):
  guest and account visits (today, yesterday, 7 and 30 days) and a 30-day
  guest chart; accounts (new and signed in today / 7 / 30 days, never signed
  in, unconfirmed) with a 30-day sign-up chart; sign-in methods; app language
  (accounts); reminder devices (which reminders, top regions by time zone,
  languages); what people use (sync, Family/Kids, MSA); SalaamStreet AI
  (including the "if paid" costs); moderation (bans, blocked emails,
  warnings, Rules agreed, admins).
- **A shorter Overview:** the alerts (flags, appeals, banner), Today
  (accounts, new, signed in, guests), the 14-day sign-up chart, SalaamStreet
  AI on/off with today's questions, and the newest accounts. Everything else
  moved to Analytics.
- Visits are visits, not people: without an id the 7- and 30-day totals can't
  tell one device from another, and the Analytics tab says so.

Tests: `tests/admin.test.js` (visit counts, refused kinds, the public can't
read the totals, Analytics numbers), `tests/e2e.test.js` (the Analytics tab and
the shorter Overview).
