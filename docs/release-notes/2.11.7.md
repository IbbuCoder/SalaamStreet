# SalaamStreet 2.11.7 — NVHS MSA: join with your school email

*NVHS students on the MSA's list are accepted automatically.*

Release 2.11.7 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` in the
> Supabase SQL editor (safe to re-run), then load the MSA's list (MSA → Manage →
> Edit the list, or the one-time SQL the maintainer has — kept out of this
> public repository).

## Join with your school email

- The MSA page now leads with **"NVHS student? You're one step away"**: tap the
  button, type your school email (…@k12.ipsd.org), type the code we email you.
  It says plainly: **use email — not "Continue with Google"** (the school blocks
  Google sign-in for school accounts).
- If the school email fits a name on the MSA's list, you're a **member straight
  away**, under the list's spelling of your name. School emails are your first
  name + the first 3 letters of your last name + 4 numbers; longer and
  hyphenated names (e.g. "Adam Al Jallad", "Zayd Faiz-balagam") match on any
  part of the last name. An address that could fit two names waits for the
  approver instead of guessing.
- Signed in with a personal account? The card says so, and **Switch to school
  email** signs you out and opens the emailed-code sign-in in one tap.
- Your school account owns your name: if someone else claimed it with the
  meeting code or a request, they're flagged for an in-person check.
- Only a **confirmed** address counts — signing in with the emailed code
  confirms it.
- Not on the list (or no school email)? The meeting code and requests work as
  in 2.11.6. Membership still runs to 1 July, and school accounts re-join by
  themselves next year while they're on the list.

## Simpler to manage

- At the top: **members**, **waiting** and **how much of the list has joined**.
- **Needs you**: only flagged and waiting requests ("All caught up" otherwise).
  Denied requests drop out of the way.
- **Show meeting code** opens the live code only when you need it.
- **MSA list**: every name with "Joined (school email / meeting code / approved
  by you)" or "Not joined yet", a search box, and one-tap remove; members who
  joined but aren't on the list are shown underneath.
- **Edit the list** is folded away until you need it.
- A note: teachers and MSA exec will be able to help manage this in a future
  update, if they want to.

## Fixes

- **Continue with Google** now always asks which account to use, instead of
  signing straight back in with the last one.

## Tests

- `tests/msa.test.js` — 4 new database tests: the email pattern for every shape
  of name; a confirmed school email on the list joins at once; no membership
  without proof (unconfirmed, other domains, look-alike domains, not on the
  list, two possible names); the school account wins its name and re-joins
  after the yearly reset.
- `tests/e2e.test.js` — 2 new browser tests: switching from a personal account
  to the school email and being accepted automatically; Google sign-in asks
  which account. The membership test covers the new Manage page.
