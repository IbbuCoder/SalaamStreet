## SalaamStreet AI (Beta)

- **For everyone with an account.** Open **SalaamStreet AI** from the menu
  (`#/ai`). Signed out, it asks you to sign in; any account with a confirmed
  email can use it.
- **Islamic questions only.** The Qur'an, tafsir, hadith, seerah, fiqh
  opinions, duas, Muslim life, and your own SalaamStreet data. Anything else
  gets a short, kind "I can only help with Islamic questions". It never
  invents verses, hadith or citations, says when it's quoting from memory,
  presents differences of opinion fairly, and sends you to a scholar for
  rulings on your own situation.
- **Commands** (type them or tap the chips): `/bookmarks`, `/ayah 2:255`,
  `/progress`, `/plan 30`, `/dua exams`, `/quiz seerah`, `/reflect`, `/help`.
  `/ayah` and `/reflect` send the real ayah text from the app.
- **Your data, if you allow it.** With "Use my app data" on, a short summary
  of your bookmarks (with notes and folders), last-read ayah, Qur'an progress,
  this week's prayers, dhikr streak and favourite duas goes with each
  question, so answers can be personal. The page only reads it.

## Fair for everyone

- **A daily allowance** for each person (20 questions to start), shown on the
  page. Failed requests don't count.
- **A waiting list.** When lots of people are asking at once (or Google is
  busy), you're put in line instead of seeing an error: "You're number 2 in
  line". Keep the page open and your question is sent automatically; you keep
  your place, and nobody can cut in. "Leave the line" gives your question
  back.
- **Private.** Conversations aren't saved anywhere — they're gone when you
  leave the page. The server keeps only counts, token numbers and costs.

## For the admin

- The AI Testing Lab (`#/lab`) gains a **beta switch** (open/closed), a
  **questions per person per day** setting, and shows **people today** and
  **who's waiting in line**. The emergency switch still stops everything.
- When the beta started, the project's limits were raised once to 1000
  requests a day and 15 a minute — change them in the Lab.

## Not in this beta

- Paid plans (Plus, Pro, Max) and payments — billing stays off.
- The AI page is in English (answers follow the language you write in).
