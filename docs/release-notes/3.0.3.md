# SalaamStreet 3.0.3 — Fairer bans

*Banned people can sign in, see why and appeal; only their account's online features are off.*

Release 3.0.3 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` (safe to
> re-run) and deploy `ai-lab` again — GitHub → Actions → "Deploy Supabase".
> Bans made before 3.0.3 are moved over automatically (and can sign in again).

## What changed

- **Signing in works while banned.** Supabase Auth's own ban (which stops
  sign-in) isn't used any more; a ban lives only in `account_bans`.
- **Online features are off for a banned account**, on the server and in the app:
  - **sync** — nothing is sent or received; the account's data stays on the
    device (the sync policies refuse it; the app doesn't try, and its status
    says "Sync is off while your account is banned");
  - **SalaamStreet AI** — `ai-lab` refuses the account (`account_is_banned`);
  - **NVHS MSA** — joining, leaving and posting are refused (a trigger on
    `msa_members` and `msa_posts`), and the MSA page and Home card aren't shown;
  - **Family** and the **Admin Panel** pages.
  Opening one of those pages shows the ban instead ("This needs your account,
  which is banned.").
- **Everything that works without an account keeps working**: prayer times,
  Qibla, the Qur'an (with downloads), duas, dhikr, hadith, stories, the
  prayer tracker, the Home banner and prayer reminders.
- **The ban screen** shows once each time the app opens (with the reason, the
  message, when it ends and the Appeal form); a red **"Your account is
  restricted"** bar at the top reopens it any time. Appeals are sent while
  signed in.
- **Strict ban for "Offensive name or email":** the app shows only the ban —
  it can't be closed — with Appeal and Sign out.
- **Expired bans end by themselves.**
- The 3.0.2 "kept sign-in token" lookup (`account_notice`) is removed; it
  isn't needed now that banned people can sign in.

## Tests

- `tests/admin.test.js` (13): a banned account can still sign in, sync, the
  MSA and SalaamStreet AI refuse it, it appeals signed in, accepting brings
  sync back; strict bans; expired bans.
- `tests/ai-lab.test.js`: a banned account can't use SalaamStreet AI.
- `tests/e2e.test.js`: banned but signed in, the bar, MSA and AI pages show
  the ban, offline pages work, no sync, appeal, reopening, reply, lifting the
  ban, the strict ban that can't be closed.
