# SalaamStreet 3.0.1 — Keeping SalaamStreet safe

*A private Admin Panel for the people who run SalaamStreet — on the website and in the iPhone app.*

Release 3.0.1 · October 2026 · In the app: About → Updates (`SS.CHANGELOG` in `js/content.js`)

> **Before deploying:** run the updated `backend/supabase-schema.sql` (safe to
> re-run) and deploy the new `admin` Edge Function and `ai-lab` again —
> GitHub → Actions → "Deploy Supabase" does all three. Until `admin` is
> deployed, the Admin Panel says it isn't deployed yet and Settings shows no
> link. Setup and details: [`backend/README-backend.md`](../../backend/README-backend.md#301--admin-panel).

## Why

Until now, finding an account meant running SQL in Supabase. When an account
with a slur in its email signed up, there was no quick way to see it, ban it
or keep a record. 3.0.1 adds one place to do all of that.

## The Admin Panel (`#/admin`)

Settings → **Admin Panel** appears only for admins (it's asked once a day per
account). It works the same on the website and in the iPhone app, which has
no address bar. Sign in with the admin **Google** account.

- **Overview** — accounts (new today and this week, signed in today / 7 / 30
  days, never signed in, banned), a 14-day sign-up chart, sign-in methods,
  what people use (synced items, reminder devices, Family, MSA), SalaamStreet
  AI today, the 5 newest accounts, and a banner when anything is flagged.
- **Users** — search by email, phone, name or account id; filter by flagged,
  banned, new, never signed in, unconfirmed or admins. Open an account to see
  its sign-in methods and dates, what it uses (counts only), its SalaamStreet
  AI record and its history in the log, then:
  **Ban** (1 / 7 / 30 days or until unbanned — signed out everywhere at once),
  **Unban**, **Sign out everywhere**, **Flag / Dismiss flag**,
  **Pause / Restore SalaamStreet AI**, or **Delete** (type the email to
  confirm; removes everything that belongs to the account).
- **Flags** — accounts waiting for review, SalaamStreet AI warnings and
  pauses, recently reviewed flags, and **sign-up screening**.
- **AI** — SalaamStreet AI's emergency switch, open/closed, and the daily
  limits, with today's usage. The model and prices stay in the AI Testing Lab.
- **MSA** — the NVHS MSA's numbers and latest announcements, with
  **Take down** for emergencies.
- **Admins** — add or remove who can open the panel.
- **Log** — every admin action, who did it and when, kept for 2 years.

## Sign-up screening

Every new account's email (the part before the @) and its name from Google or
Apple are checked against a word list — slurs to start with — after undoing
look-alikes like `1→i`, `0→o` and `@→a`. A match is **flagged** for review;
turn on **Refuse matching sign-ups** to stop them at sign-up instead.
Accounts made before 3.0.1 are checked once when the schema runs. Words are
masked in the panel until you press **Show words**.

## Safety and privacy

- The page only displays what the server returns. The `admin` Edge Function
  checks the session, a confirmed email, Google sign-in and the admin list on
  every request; every database function checks the admin list again and
  writes to the log. Nothing new is reachable with the website's public key.
- Admins can't act on their own account or on another admin's, and there is
  always at least one admin.
- Admins see counts only: never AI conversations (they aren't stored), synced
  bookmarks or notes. The log keeps a masked email (`ib***@gmail.com`) so it
  still makes sense after an account is deleted.

## iPhone app

- The app's copy of the website (`ios/SalaamStreet/WebApp`) is updated to
  3.0.1 (it was still 3.0.0-beta), build 2.
- The `ai-lab` and `admin` functions now accept the app's own origin

  Admin Panel can be reached from inside the app. Check on a device after
  deploying.
